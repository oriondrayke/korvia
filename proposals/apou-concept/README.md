# Psycho-Oncology Uganda concept

Status: independent unsolicited concept; not an official or commissioned website.

## Purpose

This prototype demonstrates a possible digital home for the Association of Psycho-Oncologists of Uganda. It is designed around four public audiences:

- patients;
- caregivers;
- cancer-care professionals; and
- psycho-oncology members, researchers and partners.

## Verified public-information basis

- Dr Rachel Kansiime Kanyangabo is identified in current reporting as founding president.
- Publicly described priorities include integrating psychosocial care into cancer management, training health workers, partnership and policy advocacy.
- Reporting describes patients, caregivers and health workers as affected audiences.
- Reporting mentions work involving Mulago National Referral Hospital and the Uganda Cancer Institute, with planned expansion of training toward Mbarara and Gulu regional cancer centres.

## Deliberately proposed—not presented as fact

- The “Psycho-Oncology Uganda” public-facing name and all visual identity.
- The support pathway, member pathway, resource structure and referral-directory direction.
- Any future service descriptions or regional availability.

## Safety and privacy choices

- The demo does not diagnose, screen or claim to provide clinical care.
- The demo does not present itself as an emergency service.
- The membership form is disabled and collects no information.
- No personal association contact information is displayed.
- Search engines are instructed not to index the concept.
