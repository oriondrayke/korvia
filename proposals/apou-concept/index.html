A clearer route to help

From distress to the right conversation.

This proposed pathway helps visitors understand the role of psycho-oncology without diagnosing them online or replacing professional care.

This website would not be an emergency service. Anyone in immediate danger should contact local emergency services or go to the nearest health facility.
  1. 01
    Recognise

    Learn how fear, anxiety, depression, grief, treatment stress and caregiver exhaustion can affect a cancer journey.

  2. 02
    Prepare

    Use simple prompts to describe what you are experiencing and prepare questions for your clinical or psychosocial care team.

  3. 03
    Connect

    Find verified services, professionals and regional partners when the association confirms its referral directory.

  4. 04
    Continue

    Access resources for coping, treatment adherence, caregiver wellbeing and support throughout treatment and follow-up.

About the emerging association

Building a home for psycho-oncology in Uganda.

Dr Rachel Kansiime Kanyangabo is publicly identified as the founding president of the Association of Psycho-Oncologists of Uganda. Her work spans clinical psychology, psycho-oncology, caregiver research, training and support within Uganda's cancer-care community.

This concept proposes a credible public platform that can grow with the association: first explaining the mission and connecting professionals, then adding approved services, a member directory, regional pathways, research and partner reporting.
