About the association

“A national platform for publishers—and for the future of books in Uganda.”

Proposed positioning statement for discussion, not an official UPA quotation.

Public sources identify the Uganda Publishers Association as an industry body and a member of the International Publishers Association. A dedicated website could make that standing easier for members, readers and partners to verify.

Concept transparency

What informed this concept

This independent demonstration was prepared from public sources. The International Publishers Association's 2025 member directory lists UPA and links to Facebook; Uganda's Ministry of Finance directory lists a public UPA contact; and a 2025 African Publishers Network conference profile identifies Martin Okia as UPA chairman. All membership data, programmes, wording and identity would require association approval before publication.
