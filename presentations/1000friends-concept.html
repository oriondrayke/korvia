Who we are

50 years of defending Oregon’s land use system

1000 Friends of Oregon was founded in 1974 by Governor Tom McCall and attorney Henry Richmond to protect Senate Bill 100 — the law that created Oregon’s iconic statewide land use planning system.

Today the organisation works in all 36 Oregon counties, promoting healthy working lands, great communities, shared prosperity and a strong democracy.

Why the redesign matters The current Drupal site, built in 2017, buries essential information in nested menus and limits multimedia storytelling. This concept turns the site into an advocacy engine: every page leads to action.
Diagnosis

Turning your current site limits into wins

Based on the RFP and a review of the current site, this concept directly addresses the biggest blockers to supporter engagement.

1. Buried information

Complex nesting hides programme pages and reports. The new architecture uses clear topic hubs and a persistent “Take Action” path.

2. Static programme pages

News, reports and updates live separately from the issues they belong to. The concept auto-links fresh content to relevant issue pages.

3. Weak action funnel

Donors and advocates drop off when the next step is unclear. Every major page ends with donate, sign-up or contact actions.
