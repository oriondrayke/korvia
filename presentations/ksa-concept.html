Kenya's eyes in orbit. A nation's future in space.

From the Malindi ground station to Taifa-1 in low Earth orbit, Kenya Space Agency coordinates the country's space ecosystem — earth observation, capacity building, research partnerships and policy leadership.

Our mandate

Coordinate. Nurture. Develop.

KSA's role is to maximise the utilisation of space opportunities for Kenya through regulation, research coordination, capacity building and international partnerships.

Moments in history
1964
Malindi Launch Base

Kenya & Italy establish a satellite launching and tracking base in Malindi.

2017
Agency Established

Kenya Space Agency created as a State Corporation under Legal Notice No. 22.

2018
1KUNS-PF Deployed

Kenya's first satellite deployed from the International Space Station.

2020
Strategic Plan

KSA launches its first Strategic Plan to deliver on its mandate.

2023
Taifa-1 Launch

Kenya's first operational earth observation satellite launches aboard SpaceX Falcon 9.

What we do

Programmes that grow a space nation.

A website should make KSA's work discoverable for students, researchers, partners and the public.

01

Earth Observation

Satellite imagery for agriculture, environment monitoring, urban planning, disaster response and natural-resource management.

02

Capacity Building

ST-MIC bootcamps, university partnerships and hands-on nanosat training for the next generation of Kenyan space professionals.

03

Policy & Regulation

Coordinating national space activities, licensing frameworks and Kenya's position in regional and international space governance.
