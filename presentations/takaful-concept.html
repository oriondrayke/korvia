About us

Pioneering ethical insurance in Kenya.

Founded in 2008 and operational from 2011, Takaful Insurance of Africa Limited provides insurance solutions consistent with Islamic finance principles.

We replace conventional risk transfer with a model of shared responsibility: members contribute to a common pool that is managed transparently and used to support one another in times of loss.

Our work spans motor, medical, family, fire and perils, burglary, goods in transit, corporate covers and index-based livestock takaful. Every product is designed to deliver real protection while avoiding interest, uncertainty and prohibited investments.

Headquartered in Kenya, we serve individuals, families, SMEs and institutions looking for finance-compliant risk management backed by professionalism and care.

Index-Based Livestock Takaful

Protecting pastoralist livelihoods against drought.

Our Index-Based Livestock Takaful (IBLT) product is designed for arid and semi-arid lands. Instead of waiting for individual loss assessments, payouts are triggered by satellite-based vegetation indices that reflect drought conditions in a given area.

This approach makes cover faster, more transparent and more affordable for pastoralist communities who depend on livestock for their income and food security.
