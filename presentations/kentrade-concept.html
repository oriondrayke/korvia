Our mandate

Facilitate trade. Digitise processes. Reduce friction.

Established in 2011 as a State Corporation under the National Treasury and Planning, KenTrade is mandated to facilitate cross-border trade and implement the Kenya TradeNet System.

The National Electronic Single Window System — known as the Kenya TradeNet System — allows traders to submit regulatory documents once through a single entry point, replacing multiple paper runs between government agencies and speeding up cargo clearance.

Key work

One platform, many moving parts.

KenTrade's programmes touch every stage of the trade journey — from information access to cargo release.

01

Kenya TradeNet System

The National Electronic Single Window System lets traders lodge standardised information and documents with a single entry point to fulfil regulatory requirements.

02

InfoTrade Kenya Portal

A trade information gateway that consolidates procedures, regulations, tariffs and market data to help exporters and importers plan shipments.

03

Cargo Clearance Reform

Working with border agencies to reduce the number of steps, duplications and physical visits needed to clear goods through Kenyan ports and borders.

04

Process Automation

Moving permits, licences, payments and declarations from paper-based workflows to connected digital systems across the trade ecosystem.

The single-window journey

From many desks to one entry point.

Before the single-window approach, traders navigated separate agencies, forms and physical counters. Kenya TradeNet consolidates these touchpoints into one digital workflow.
