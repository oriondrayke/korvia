For families & caregivers

Clear pathways when you need help.

A concept for a website that respects how stressful it can feel to search for support — with plain language, next steps, and easy ways to reach a person.

What this section could include

Simple intake guides, frequently asked questions, eligibility checklists, and a prominent phone number and email for personal assistance.

  • Step-by-step "How do I get started?" guide
  • FAQs about services, costs, and eligibility
  • Downloadable resource sheets
  • Contact form with a quick response promise
  • Caregiver-specific support page

Designed for reassurance

Large text, calm colors, and straightforward navigation so visitors of any comfort level with technology can find what they need without overwhelm.

  • Prominent phone number on every page
  • Plain-language service descriptions
  • Print-friendly pages for sharing
  • Clear calls to action with human follow-up
  • Mobile-friendly for families on the go

Accessibility-first design

Built to be easy for everyone.

Older adults and caregivers deserve a website that meets them where they are — with readability, contrast, and simplicity at the center.

Aa

Large, legible type

Generous font sizes, comfortable line spacing, and clear headings help every visitor read with ease.

◐

High contrast

Strong color contrast between text and backgrounds supports vision differences and outdoor screen use.

≡

Simple navigation

Fewer menu levels, descriptive labels, and persistent contact links make the site predictable and calm.

✓

WCAG focus

Keyboard-friendly layouts, visible focus indicators, and screen-reader considerations throughout.
