About MTC

Created by the Missouri General Assembly. Governed by a 15-member Board.

The Missouri Technology Corporation is a public-private partnership created by the Missouri General Assembly, led by a 15-member Board, and dedicated to growing the state's high-tech economy.

Brand refresh direction

A visual identity built for trust, momentum and scale.

A modern MTC brand system would signal institutional credibility while feeling as ambitious as the companies it supports.

01

Logo system

A refined mark that balances Missouri heritage with forward motion — primary lockup, stacked variant, icon-only mark and clear-space rules.

02

Color & typography

Deep navy anchors institutional trust. Electric blue signals tech momentum. Warm gold adds optimism and highlights strategic priorities.

03

Brand standards

Usage guidelines for print, digital, presentations and partner co-branding — ensuring consistency across every MTC touchpoint.

Website & digital experience

A platform that makes MTC's work discoverable, useful and measurable.

The new MTC website would move beyond a static brochure into a living resource for entrepreneurs, investors, researchers and policymakers.

Built on a modern CMS, the experience would feature a searchable grant award database, grantee profiles, program showcases, interactive timelines and a portfolio directory.

Data visualizations would bring MTC's impact to life — clear, credible and always tied to publicly available information.

Accessibility & performance

Built to serve every Missourian, on any device.

A public institution's website must be fast, accessible and secure. The proposed MTC platform would meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards and follow ADA compliance best practices.

SEO-ready architecture, readable typography, keyboard navigation and scalable hosting would ensure the site remains reliable during funding announcements, application windows and media attention.
