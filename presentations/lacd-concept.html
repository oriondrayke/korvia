Who we are

A Liberian NGO with national reach and community roots

Founded in 2013 and based in Monrovia, the Liberia Agency for Community Development (LACD) works to improve the lives of vulnerable communities through sustainable, locally owned programmes.

Our work spans sustainable agriculture, food security, climate resilience, education, health and nutrition, women’s empowerment, youth development, governance, clean energy and economic inclusion.

Why this website matters now After more than a decade of programme delivery, LACD needs a digital presence that matches the scale of its impact — one that donors, partners, government and communities can trust, navigate and act on.

Diagnosis

Turning three critical gaps into strengths

Based on LACD’s own tender objective and the wider NGO landscape, this concept directly addresses the organisation’s biggest digital weaknesses.

1. Institutional invisibility

LACD has almost no searchable web presence. Donors and partners cannot verify credibility, review past work or understand geographic reach. The new site becomes the single source of truth.

2. Disconnected achievements

Years of programme results live in reports, photos and memories. The concept turns these into tagged, searchable stories that auto-link to programme pages, funders and media.

3. Weak donor journey

Without a donation page, newsletter funnel or clear calls to action, interested supporters drop off. The concept builds a supporter pathway from first visit to recurring donor.

Designed to win trust and funding

Every design decision is built around the people LACD serves and the stakeholders it must convince: donors, government, partners and communities.

Mobile-first design: most Liberian users browse on phones
Fast loading even on slower networks through lightweight code
CMS training so staff update news, reports and galleries without developers
SSL, backups, anti-spam and analytics as standard
