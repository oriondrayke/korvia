A national blueprint, delivered in stages.

Kenya Vision 2030 is the country's long-term development agenda to transform it into a newly industrialising, upper-middle-income nation with a high quality of life for all Kenyans.

Our mandate

Coordinate the blueprint. Track progress. Deliver results.

The Vision 2030 Delivery Secretariat drives implementation through flagship projects, Medium-Term Plans, monitoring and evaluation, public-private partnerships, and knowledge-sharing across government and industry.

Three pillars, one goal
E

Economic & Macro

Building a competitive, prosperous economy through flagship infrastructure, manufacturing, tourism, agriculture, financial services and trade.

S

Social Pillar

Investing in education, health, housing, water and sanitation, and social protection to create a just and cohesive society.

P

Political Pillar

Strengthening governance, rule of law, public-service delivery, security and devolution for all Kenyans.

From blueprint to reality

The delivery journey.

Vision 2030 unfolds through successive Medium-Term Plans, each translating high-level goals into concrete programmes, projects and measurable indicators.

01
2008

Vision 2030 Launched

Kenya's long-term development blueprint is unveiled, built on three pillars and the foundations of infrastructure, energy, ICT and governance.

02
2009

Delivery Secretariat Established

The Vision 2030 Delivery Secretariat is created as a SAGA to spearhead and coordinate national implementation.

03
2008–2022

First Three Medium-Term Plans

MTP I, II and III translate the blueprint into actionable priorities, flagship projects and sector outcomes.

04
2023–2027

MTP IV: Bottom-Up Economic Transformation

The fourth Medium-Term Plan focuses on economic turnaround, job creation and inclusive growth through value chains.

How delivery happens

Programmes that move the plan forward.

The Secretariat's work spans coordination, communication, monitoring and partnership building to keep flagship projects on track.

01

Flagship Projects

Identifying, prioritising and tracking high-impact national projects across economic, social and political sectors.

02

Monitoring & Evaluation

Measuring progress against Medium-Term Plan indicators and producing public reports on implementation status.

03

Public-Private Partnerships

Bringing together government, private sector and development partners to finance and deliver strategic initiatives.

04

Communication & Marketing

Building public awareness of Vision 2030, celebrating milestones and keeping citizens informed about national priorities.

05

Knowledge Sharing

Workshops, forums and capacity-building events that spread implementation lessons across ministries and counties.

06

Medium-Term Planning

Coordinating the design and review of successive MTPs to keep the 2030 agenda focused, funded and achievable.

Foundations of transformation

Infrastructure, ICT, energy and governance under every outcome.

Long-term development depends on reliable foundations. The Vision 2030 agenda links flagship projects in transport, energy, water, ICT and public-service reform to the three national pillars.
