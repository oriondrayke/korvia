Who we are

A trusted catalyst for inclusive finance

We use the Making Markets Work for the Poor (M4P) approach to identify systemic constraints, partner with public and private actors, and test solutions that can scale across Kenya.

Long-term perspective

Market change takes time. We stay with complex problems long enough to move from pilots to policy.

People at the centre

Women, micro-entrepreneurs, rural households and youth are the focus of every programme we design.

Evidence-led change

From FinAccess national surveys to county-level diagnostics, we put data at the heart of decision-making.

Programmes

Where we focus our energy

Our current work spans six interconnected areas that together shape a more inclusive financial system.

01 Digital finance

Expanding safe, affordable digital payment rails and responsible agent networks for households and small businesses.

02 Credit markets

Improving access to productive credit for MSEs, farmers and women through better data, guarantees and market infrastructure.

03 Climate & green finance

Developing financing mechanisms that help households and enterprises adapt to climate risk and invest in green livelihoods.

04 Women & financial inclusion

Closing the persistent gender gap in access, usage and control of financial services across Kenya.

05 Housing & land

Unlocking housing finance, tenure security and construction ecosystems for low and middle-income families.

06 Real economy

Strengthening market systems in agriculture, trade and MSME growth so finance flows to productive activity.
