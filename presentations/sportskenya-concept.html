About Sports Kenya

Powering sport, from grassroots to stadium.

Sports Kenya is a State Corporation in Kenya responsible for developing, managing and marketing quality sport and recreation facilities, services and programmes.

Our mandate is to make sport and recreation accessible, professional and sustainable across Kenya. We manage public facilities, support event delivery, build partnerships and create pathways for talent to grow from local grounds to international arenas.

Our vision

To be a leading sports and recreation organisation in Africa — recognised for world-class facilities, thriving programmes and a culture of athletic excellence.

What we do

Programmes that move the nation.

Sports Kenya's work spans facilities management, recreation, events, partnerships and talent development.

01

Facility Management

Stewardship of national and regional stadia, sports grounds and recreation centres to keep them safe, accessible and event-ready.

02

Events & Hosting

Enabling local and international sporting events through venue services, event support and partnership with sports federations.

03

Talent Development

Creating pathways for athletes, coaches and technical officials through training programmes, access to facilities and competitive opportunities.

04

Recreation Services

Running public recreation centres and programmes that encourage fitness, wellness and community participation in sport.

05

Partnerships

Collaborating with government agencies, county governments, sports bodies and private partners to grow Kenya's sports ecosystem.

06

Marketing & Commercialisation

Promoting facilities and programmes to users, sponsors and event organisers to ensure sustainability and wider reach.

Flagship facilities

Home to Kenya's greatest sporting moments.

Sports Kenya manages some of the country's most iconic venues, including Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani, and Nyayo National Stadium — facilities that have hosted world-class athletics, football and cultural events.
