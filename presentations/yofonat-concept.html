01 · Why YOFONAT

Young people are not tomorrow. They are now.

YOFONAT creates routes for young people to participate in the decisions, systems and solutions that shape their lives—especially those too often excluded from public leadership.

The organisation's public work connects civic participation with gender equality, sexual and reproductive health, mental wellbeing, climate resilience and practical green livelihoods.

02 · What we work on

One generation. Many fronts.

A programme architecture that makes YOFONAT's range understandable without losing the connections between youth, health, justice and climate.

01Climate + livelihoods

Waste GOLD

Equipping young people in Lumbadzi with agroecology, organic-fertiliser production and practical green skills for climate-resilient livelihoods, with support from Malawi Environmental Endowment Trust.

02Governance

Youth voice

Building leadership, strengthening civic participation and opening meaningful routes into community and national decision-making.

03Health

Healthy futures

Supporting youth-centred SRHR education, mental-health awareness and informed, healthy choices in communities and schools.

04Rights + inclusion

Gender equality

Advocating for equal opportunity and safer, more inclusive spaces for girls, women, young people and people with disabilities.

04 · Evidence and learning

From tokenism to participation.

A 2026 academic case study examined YOFONAT's community-level governance work. It found the organisation combines digital advocacy, coalition-building and direct engagement to help young people enter formal governance spaces, while still facing funding and institutional barriers.

A dedicated evidence centre could bring research, project reports, public positions and learning resources together for communities, partners and funders.
