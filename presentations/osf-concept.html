Community care spotlight

Dignity should live close to home.

Community palliative care can help families understand serious illness, connect with support and reduce avoidable suffering. OSF's publicly documented orientation work in rural Lilongwe points to a model built around local leaders and health workers.

The last-mile model

Change moves in circles.

A useful website should make the method as clear as the mission: start with community knowledge, build practical capacity, connect people to systems, then carry evidence into policy.

Listen locally

Begin with lived experience, barriers and priorities identified by communities.

Equip people

Turn knowledge into practical skills for leaders, volunteers, families and advocates.

Connect care

Strengthen the routes between people, public services, civil society and partners.

Influence systems

Use field evidence and coalition voices to inform policy and long-term investment.

Men's health · March 2025

From a missing conversation to a shared agenda.

OSF partnered in a Lilongwe stakeholder meeting designed to begin a wider debate on men's health—covering evidence gaps, prostate cancer, service access, policy and future action.
